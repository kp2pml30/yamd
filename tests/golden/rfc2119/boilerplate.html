The key words MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, RECOMMENDED, NOT RECOMMENDED, MAY, and OPTIONAL in this document are to be interpreted as described in RFC 2119.

The server MUST reject expired tokens and SHALL NOT reuse a nonce. It SHOULD rotate keys; it MAY cache the JWKS.

MAY
Truly optional: an implementation may choose to include the item because a particular market or situation calls for it, or to omit it; interoperating peers must handle both. [1]
MUST
An absolute requirement of the specification. [1]
MUST NOT
An absolute prohibition of the specification. [1]
SHOULD
Recommended: there may exist valid reasons in particular circumstances to ignore it, but the full implications must be understood and carefully weighed first. [1]
SHOULD NOT
Not recommended: there may exist valid reasons in particular circumstances when the behavior is acceptable or even useful, but the full implications should be understood and the case weighed before choosing it.
